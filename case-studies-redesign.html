Robinhood

2026
Robinhood 01

High-Velocity Usability Testing of a Prediction Market Educational Module

Compressed a multi-week research cycle into under 48 hours—validating an educational module for event contracts ahead of the World Cup.

Problem: One week before launch, Robinhood had only static images to test and no time for a traditional study—leadership needed large-sample proof before greenlighting.

Under 48 hours turnaround 2026 ↗
Robinhood 02

Onboarding Optimization & Strategic Retention in Prediction Markets

Used survival analysis and loss-aversion messaging to cut post-rejection drop-off.

Problem: 1 in 5 first trades was rejected, and 90% of those users vanished afterward—nobody knew exactly when they gave up or how to win them back.

~$17M monthly revenue retained 2026 ↗
Robinhood 03

Engineering an AI-Agentic UX Research Pipeline (ResearchOps Automation)

Built autonomous Claude + Figma agents that compressed a 30-day research cycle to 1 week or less.

Problem: A single project took up to 30 days, and almost none of that time was research—planning, approvals, and a 4-5 day wait on user lists ate the calendar.

4x concurrent study capacity 2026 ↗

FanDuel

2021–2025
FanDuel 04

Scaled Product Benchmarking, Responsible Gaming & Research Operations

Scaled UX research into a 32-person division, built AI research pipelines, and grew Responsible Gaming into a regulator-approved Sentiment Scale.

Problem: Five product lines had no shared way to measure UX, research was a 2-person team, and at-risk gambling behavior had no validated way to be measured.

75% faster turnaround, 32-person division 2021–2025 ↗
FanDuel 05

Acquisition Offers & First-Bet Activation

A requested deep dive on acquisition offers that boosted first-bet conversion and showed the lower-stakes offer won on ROI and loyalty.

Problem: Leadership didn't know which acquisition offer actually earned its payout before state launches where first impressions would decide market share.

Higher first-bet conversion 2024 ↗
FanDuel 06

Wallet Deposit Optimization

Diagnosed 65% wallet-setup drop-off and redesigned KYC with progressive disclosure and trust framing.

Problem: 65% of new sign-ups abandoned at wallet setup even though every prior step felt seamless—was it a motivation problem or a trust problem?

150K+ users, ~$8M Q1 handle 2025 ↗
FanDuel 07

Cross-Sell Growth Across FanDuel Products

Architected continuous CX measurement to diagnose a cross-sell plateau—unlocking projected LTV growth.

Problem: Only ~10% of customers used more than one product line, and that number hadn't moved in a year—marketing had no read on why.

2x acquisition in 90 days, $500M LTV 2025 ↗

NFL Fantasy 08

Fantasy D2C Ideation — Tools Package Monetization

Proved the NFL could monetize Fantasy directly without disrupting free gameplay—via a mid-season Tools Package.

Problem: Fantasy had never charged players a dollar, but data suggested its most active users had unmet needs they might pay to solve.

New monetization path validated 2020 ↗
NFL Fantasy 09

NFL+ Direct-to-Consumer Packaging Research

Evaluated alternate NFL+ packaging configurations to inform pricing and bundling for the league's first mobile subscription offering.

Problem: The NFL had five possible packaging configurations for its first mobile subscription and no data on which one fans would pay for.

Informed NFL+ launch pricing 2020 ↗

Intel TrueView Design Studio

Fan-validated go/no-go signal 2019 ↗
