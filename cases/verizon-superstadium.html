Testing a Next-Generation In-Stadium Fan Experience

In-stadium fans didn’t want a second screen—they wanted the stadium itself to become the interface.

Situation

  • The league’s innovation lab wanted next-generation ways to deepen how fans engage during live games, especially inside the stadium.
  • The carrier’s 5G network made new experiences possible: real-time AR overlays and multi-angle viewing from your seat.

Task

  • Lead usability and field research on the carrier-powered 5G, AR, and VR fan experiences built for in-stadium use.
  • Turn the findings into a concrete product direction the lab could formalize with the carrier.

Action

  • Ran cognitive task analysis and in-stadium field research during live events — capturing real fan behavior at a major-league stadium during a live game.
  • Ran prototype and usability testing on immersive AR hardware alongside the carrier’s innovation team.
  • Shaped the product direction: multi-angle live and replay views, live-stats AR overlays, and in-stadium navigation running over 5G.

Result

  • The research helped seed the in-stadium experience, which is now built into the league’s official app and sits at the center of a $1 billion-plus partnership between the league and the carrier.
  • Fans used it at the sport’s biggest event. At the league championship game, roughly 60% of attendees were actively connected to the experience.
