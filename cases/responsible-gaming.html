Measuring the Invisible: A Validated Scale for Responsible Gaming

Core Products & Experiences
Role
UX Researcher (scale developer, study lead)
Timeline
Multi-phase (scale development → validation → deployment)
Methods
Scale development · Exploratory & confirmatory factor analysis · Psychometric validation · Behavioral risk-tool design

Situation

  • The company had risk-detection and player-protection tools already live in the app, but no validated way to measure whether they actually reduced at-risk play.
  • Regulators were raising the bar on what counted as adequate player protection — having tools wasn’t a defensible answer without a measure showing they worked.
  • The signals available (support contacts, self-exclusion requests) were lagging indicators: they showed harm after it happened, not risk before it did.

Task

  • Build and validate an instrument that could measure responsible-gaming sentiment and behavior directly, not just track downstream harm after the fact.
  • Use that measure to guide which risk-prevention tools to build or refine, and prove whether they actually moved behavior.
  • Get the measure to a rigor bar that regulators and the business would both trust.

Action

  • Generated and refined an item pool grounded in the psychology of risk perception and self-control, then ran it through the standard scale-development pipeline: pilot testing, exploratory and confirmatory factor analysis, and reliability testing.
  • Validated the resulting Responsible Gaming Sentiment Scale against real behavioral outcomes, not just self-report.
  • Used the validated scale to evaluate and refine the in-app risk-detection and intervention tools, iterating on which prompts and limits genuinely shifted behavior.
  • Folded the scale into ongoing measurement, tracked alongside usability and loyalty metrics as part of the broader benchmarking program.

Result

  • The share of customers meeting regulatory player-protection standards rose 14 percentage points — the highest level the program had reached. The lift tracked directly to the risk tools the scale had been used to refine.
  • Gave the company a defensible, validated way to show its player-protection tools worked — not just that they existed.
  • Became the measurement backbone referenced in the org’s broader benchmarking program, tracked alongside usability and loyalty scores.
